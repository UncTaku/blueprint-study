# Blueprint Study

Offline drills for print reading: alphabet of lines, GD&T symbols, dimensions and callouts.

**Use it:** open the site on your phone → browser menu → **Add to Home Screen**. Works offline after the first visit.

## Decks
- **Alphabet of lines**: name the line from its drawing, plus thick/thin and what lines mean.
- **GD&T symbols**: the 14 characteristic symbols plus MMC / LMC / RFS / projected zone.
- **Dimensions & callouts**: counterbore, countersink, depth, basic vs reference, reading hole callouts and frames.

## Modes
- **Quiz**: pick from 4. The wrong answers are deliberately the ones people confuse.
- **Flashcards**: think of the answer, flip, then grade yourself. If you hesitated, call it a miss.

Missed cards come back 3 cards later until you get them.

## Editing
- Card data: `cards.js`. Drawings: `art.js` (SVG, so symbols look right on every phone).
- **After changing any file, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js`**, or installed phones keep the old cached copy.
